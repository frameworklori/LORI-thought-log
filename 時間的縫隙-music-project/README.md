## 🧠 Concept

This piece explores the contrast between cosmic scale and human emotion.  
Even in an infinite universe, connection remains the strongest force.

It reflects on:

- Separation caused by disaster and war  
- The fragility of human life  
- The possibility of reunion beyond time and space
